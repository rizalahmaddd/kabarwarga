# DESIGN.md

> Direction written by the agent at the owner's request (owner chose "papan pengumuman balai warga").
> Agent-authored direction tends toward default taste; revise freely.

## Identity
A neighbourhood notice board (papan pengumuman balai warga), moved online. Residents of all ages,
many older and on phones, check what's new and whether their dues are recorded. Pengurus (admins)
post notices and record payments, often right after collecting cash door to door.

## Personality
Warm, plain-spoken, local. Feels like paper pinned to a board and a treasurer's ledger with rubber
stamps, not like a SaaS dashboard.

## Palette
- Paper (base): `#f5efe2`, card paper `#fffbf2`, rule lines `#d9cfbb`
- Ink (text): `#2b2721`, muted ink `#5e5647`
- Daun (core, primary actions + "lunas"): `#2f5d3a`
- Terakota (single accent, only for pinned/important notices): `#a8482a`

## Typography
- Headings: Bitter (slab serif, reads like printed/typed notices on a board)
- Body: Source Sans 3 (humanist, open letterforms, plain numerals so money amounts read cleanly; Atkinson Hyperlegible was tried but its slashed zero looked odd in Rp amounts)

## Motif
Rubber stamp: bordered, slightly rotated uppercase label. Used for post category and the "LUNAS"
mark in the dues ledger. Pinned notices carry a push-pin mark (real state: pinned).

## Layout
Content first. Home = pinned notices, upcoming activities, this month's dues progress, latest news.
Admin home = the payment recording form (the job pengurus do most).

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Theme
Light only: the identity is paper on a board. No dark mode.
